Lunmi Privacy Policy
Version 1.0 | Last updated October 16, 2026
Review draft. Publication requires verification of the practices described below.

1. About this Policy

Lunmi is a social space for sharing lanterns, hanging wishes, exchanging blessings and replies, joining Voice Rooms, and continuing established conversations through messages and video calls. This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Lunmi, referred to as "Lunmi," "we," "us," or "our," handles personal information in connection with these services.

Contact us about privacy, safety, account support, or this Policy at lunmi_user@gmail.com. Please use the subject "Privacy Request" for a data rights request and "Safety Report" for an abuse report. This Policy explains our practices; it does not replace a permission prompt or any separate consent required by law.

Lunmi is for people aged 18 or older. Do not create an account or use the service if you are under 18.

2. Information You Provide

Account and profile information. We process the information needed to create and maintain your account, such as your email address, username, authentication information, and account identifier. If you add an avatar, biography, or city to your profile, we process that information as well. A display name does not make your account or activity unidentifiable to us.

Content and communications. We process lanterns, wishes, blessings, initial replies, ongoing messages, and any photos, audio, or other supported media you choose to submit. We also process information associated with this content, such as its author, recipients, publication time, and moderation status. Information you share may reveal sensitive matters about you or others. Avoid posting information that you do not want the intended audience to know, and do not disclose another person's private information without a lawful basis.

Voice Rooms and calls. Providing live audio and video involves processing the media you transmit and connection information such as room or call identifiers, participants, connection times, duration, and technical quality information. Other participants receive the audio or video you choose to share. See Section 6 for recording and audience information.

Safety and support information. We process reports, blocking choices, feedback, appeals, support correspondence, and evidence you submit. Reports may include another user's content, relevant message excerpts, account identifiers, and contextual information needed to investigate.

Purchases. If you purchase Lantern Coins, we process transaction identifiers, purchased products, transaction status, Coin balances, and spending records needed to validate purchases and deliver the selected visibility or placement service. Apple handles payment information for purchases made through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card number from Apple for those purchases.

3. Information Collected When You Use Lunmi

We process technical information needed to connect to, operate, and secure the service. Depending on the feature used, this includes IP address, device and operating system information, app version, language settings, timestamps, connection failures, and diagnostic or security logs. We also process service activity such as publishing a lantern, opening a conversation, joining a room, sending a blessing, submitting a report, or using a paid placement.

An IP address can indicate an approximate location. A city you enter manually is different from device location information. If you choose an available location-based feature and grant location permission, the information available to that feature depends on your device settings, including whether you allow approximate or precise location. The permission prompt must explain the specific purpose. We do not publish device coordinates as part of your profile; a city you choose to display may be visible to others.

Local storage and similar app technologies keep you signed in, store preferences, preserve necessary app state, and support security. Optional analytics, advertising, or tracking require an accurate additional disclosure and any consent or choices required for the particular technology. This Policy does not itself authorize tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Device Permissions and Your Choices

Camera. Used when you choose to take supported photos or participate with video in a call. Denying access prevents camera-dependent actions, rather than unrelated text features.

Microphone. Used when you choose to speak in a Voice Room, transmit audio in a call, or create supported audio content. You can mute yourself or leave a room or call. Denying microphone access prevents speaking or recording through the relevant feature.

Photos. Used to select or attach supported media, such as an avatar or an image accompanying your content. Access is limited by the selection or library permission you grant. Choosing one photo is not permission to use unrelated photos for other purposes.

Notifications. Used, with your permission, to alert you to activity such as replies and calls. You can turn notifications off in device settings. A notification preview may be visible to anyone who can see your device, so review your device preview settings.

Location. Requested only for an available feature that needs it, with an explanation at the time of the request. You can decline or withdraw access. Where a manual city option is available, you may use it instead.

You can manage permissions through your device's settings. Revoking a permission stops future access through that permission; it does not automatically remove information previously submitted. You may separately request deletion as explained below. We do not make unrelated service access conditional on an unnecessary permission.

5. Why We Use Information

We use relevant information to:

- Create accounts, authenticate users, maintain sessions, and provide account support.
- Publish and discover lanterns and wishes, deliver replies and blessings, and apply the rule allowing one initial reply before the lantern owner responds to open a private conversation.
- Deliver messages, Voice Rooms, and calls that participants choose to use.
- Validate purchases, maintain Coin records, and deliver the selected Lantern Boost or Wish Tree placement.
- Apply blocking choices; detect spam, fraud, exploitation, and other abuse; investigate reports; moderate content; and handle appeals.
- Diagnose failures, maintain service performance, and address security incidents.
- Communicate service notices and respond to support or privacy requests.
- Comply with legal duties and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not treat your acceptance of the Terms of Service as blanket permission for every form of personal information processing. Any materially different use requires an appropriate legal basis and notice, and consent when required.

6. Who Can See Content and How Live Media Is Handled

Lanterns and Wish Tree content are intended for discovery by other users. Associated profile information and interactions may be visible within the relevant feature. Publish only information suitable for that audience. A paid visibility or placement choice can affect how content is presented; it does not purchase another person's attention or permission to contact them.

An initial lantern reply is delivered to the lantern owner. An ongoing private conversation opens after the owner replies. Private messages are not published as a public feed. Nevertheless, a recipient can copy or disclose what you send, and limited access by authorized personnel or service providers may be necessary for delivery, support, safety investigations, or legal compliance. The word "private" does not promise that messages are inaccessible to the service operator or that end-to-end encryption is implemented.

Voice Room participants may hear what you say and see your room participation. In a video call, the other participant receives the video and audio you choose to transmit. You may decline a call, mute, disable your camera where available, or leave. Use reporting and blocking tools if another person behaves abusively.

Live transmission is distinct from storing a recording. Any Lunmi feature that records, transcribes, or retains live conversation content must provide a clear notice of what is captured, why it is captured, who receives it, and its retention, together with the permissions and consent required before capture. This Policy does not grant general consent to record a room or call. Other participants may use tools outside our control to capture content; such conduct remains subject to our Terms and applicable law.

Other users. We disclose content and profile information to the audiences described in Section 6 and to the recipients you choose.

Service providers. Hosting, storage, media delivery, real-time communication, authentication, security, moderation, and support providers may process the information needed for their assigned functions. For example, a real-time communication provider processes live media and connection information to connect participants. Providers acting on our behalf must be bound by suitable confidentiality, security, purpose, and data protection obligations, providing protection consistent with this Policy and applicable requirements.

Apple. Apple processes App Store purchases under its own terms and privacy notices. We exchange the transaction information needed to verify purchases and address payment problems.

Legal and safety disclosures. We may disclose information when required by law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request, or when necessary and legally permitted to address a serious safety threat, protect rights, or investigate fraud or abuse. We limit disclosures to information relevant to the purpose.

Business changes. If the service is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset transfer, relevant information may be transferred subject to applicable law, appropriate safeguards, and any required notice or consent.

Other disclosures. We will obtain consent or provide additional notices and choices where a disclosure requires them. Information that has been effectively de-identified may be used for service statistics; we do not describe merely pseudonymous or account-linked records as anonymous.

8. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for the period needed for the purpose for which it was collected, taking into account the type of information, your account status, legal duties, safety risks, disputes, and whether the purpose can be achieved with less information.

- Account and profile records are maintained while needed to operate your account, then deleted or de-identified following account deletion unless a specific retention exception applies.
- Published content and communications are retained as needed to provide the feature and respect deletion choices. Removing content from discovery and erasing associated records are different steps.
- Live media is processed to connect participants. Any separate retained recording or transcript requires the specific disclosure described in Section 6.
- Purchase and Coin records may be retained for transaction validation, accounting, fraud prevention, refund handling, and legally required recordkeeping.
- Reports, relevant evidence, and enforcement records may be retained for safety investigations, appeals, preventing repeat abuse, and legal obligations.
- Technical logs and backups are retained according to their operational purpose. Residual backup copies are protected, excluded from ordinary use, and deleted or overwritten through the backup cycle. If a backup is restored, applicable deletion requests must be reapplied.

You can initiate account deletion through Settings > Delete Account. Uninstalling Lunmi or signing out does not delete your account. You may also contact lunmi_user@gmail.com if you cannot access the deletion control. We may take proportionate steps to verify that a request relates to your account.

After a valid deletion request, we delete or de-identify account-linked information without undue delay, subject to applicable legal deadlines and limited exceptions. Where an exception applies, we retain only the information needed for that exception, restrict its use, and remove it when the exception ends. A report or dispute does not justify keeping every account record indefinitely. Copies already retained independently by another user may be outside our control. Where deletion or de-identification can be applied to service-controlled copies, we apply it as required by law.

9. Security and Processing Locations

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and risks, including access controls and measures to protect service communications. No service can guarantee that every transmission or stored record will be secure. Keep your credentials confidential and contact us if you suspect account misuse.

Service infrastructure and providers may process information outside your country of residence. Where cross-border rules apply, the relevant processing must meet those rules, including an appropriate transfer mechanism and additional safeguards where required. You can contact us for information about processing locations and safeguards relevant to your information. We do not claim that an unspecified transfer is compliant merely because you use the app.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you and the circumstances, you may request access to your information, a copy of it, correction, deletion, portability, restriction of processing, or objection to particular processing. You may also have rights to withdraw consent, opt out of sale, sharing for cross-context advertising or targeted advertising where applicable, limit certain sensitive information uses, and appeal a refused request. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Send requests to lunmi_user@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request." Please identify the account and the action requested, without sending passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We use proportionate verification and respond within the period required by the applicable law. If we cannot fulfill a request, we explain the applicable reason and any available review or appeal route. An authorized agent may act for you where the law permits, subject to appropriate verification.

Where European or UK data protection rules apply, the relevant legal bases are performance of our contract for necessary account, messaging, and purchase services; legitimate interests for proportionate security, abuse prevention, and service reliability, subject to a balancing assessment; legal obligations for mandatory compliance; and consent for processing that requires it. Sensitive information requires a separate applicable condition; publishing sensitive content is not general consent to unrelated profiling. You may complain to your competent data protection authority and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Withdrawal of consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

If you believe a user is underage or that we have collected information from someone under 18, report the account in the app or email lunmi_user@gmail.com. We investigate credible reports, restrict underage access, and delete associated information as appropriate, while retaining limited information only where a lawful safety or legal reason requires it.
